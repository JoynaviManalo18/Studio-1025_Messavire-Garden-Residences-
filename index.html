The City

Bacolod, the City of Smiles.

Bacolod grew up on sugar. For over a century, sprawling haciendas across Negros Occidental built the fortunes behind its grand ancestral homes — and that heritage is still visible today, from the sugar-baron mansion known as The Ruins to the heritage houses of nearby Silay.

Its nickname comes from the MassKara Festival, born in 1980 as an act of collective resilience after tragedy struck the city — locals responded by painting on smiling masks and taking to the streets, and the tradition has run every October since. That same warmth carries into everyday life here: unhurried mornings, generous hospitality, and a food scene built around Bacolod's famous chicken inasal.

Landmark

The Ruins

A grand ancestral mansion shell in nearby Talisay, known for its gardens and sunset views — one of the region's most photographed spots.

Culture

Negros Museum

A heritage museum in a 1925 Art Deco building telling the story of Negros' sugar industry through art, artifacts and personal histories.

Park

Capitol Lagoon Park

A shaded, well-kept park right in the city center — a relaxed spot for a morning walk or an early evening stroll.

M
Food

Manokan Country

The go-to strip for authentic chicken inasal — Bacolod's famous grilled chicken, best enjoyed with rice and soy-calamansi dip.

Shopping

Malls on Lacson Street

Robinsons Place and nearby malls for shopping, dining and everyday errands, both within easy reach of the unit.

Day Trip

Mambukal Mountain Resort

Hot springs, waterfalls and a canopy walk at the foot of Mt. Kanlaon — a popular half-day escape from the city.

C
Day Trip

Campuestohan Highland Resort

Pools, a zipline and cool highland air — a favorite for families and groups looking for a full day out.

Heritage

Silay City

A short drive away, known for its well-preserved ancestral houses and old-world charm — great for a slow afternoon walk.

Festival

MassKara Festival

If your stay falls in October, don't miss Bacolod's famous "City of Smiles" street festival of masks, music and dancing.
